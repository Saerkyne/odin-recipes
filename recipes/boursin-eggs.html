Boursin Egg Bake

Recipe Description

This is a simple four ingredient "casserole" that can be made for an easy breakfast or lunch.

Original Source: AllRecipes

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • 1 (5.3 ounce) package of Boursin Garlic and Fine Herbs chese
  • 8 large eggs
  • salt and ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400F. Spray a 9x9 baking dish with cooking spray.
  2. Place the Boursin in the center of the dish.
  3. Crack all 8 eggs into the dish around the Boursin.
  4. Bake until the egg white is cooked through, about 15 minutes. Yolks may be slightly runny.
  5. Mash the eggs and Boursin together with a fork, then season to taste with salt and pepepr.
