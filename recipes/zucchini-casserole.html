Zucchini Casserole

Recipe Description

Zucchini, cornflakes, and bell pepper. What?

Original Source: AllRecipes

Ingredients

  • 8 cups diced zucchini
  • 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 cup cornflakes cereal (can be substituted with bread crumbs)
  • 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F. Lightly oil 9x13 baking dish.
  2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix thoroughly.
  3. Spread mixture evenly into baking dish.
  4. Bake until top is golden brown, about 45 minutes.
