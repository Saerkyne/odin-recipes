World's Best Lasagna

Recipe Description

This recipe comes from AllRecipes user John Chandler, who submitted it 20 years ago. It is a basic meat lasagna, but adds sugar and a blend of cheeses for depth.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
  • 3/4 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 cup minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 (6.5 ounce) cans canned tomato sauce
  • 2 (6 ounce) cans tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp white sugar
  • 4 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, divided
  • 1 1/2 tsp dried basil
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, divided
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 3/4 pound sliced mozzarella cheese
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Brown all together.
  2. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and water.
  3. Season with sugar, half the parsley, basil, 1 tsp of salt, Italian seasoning, and pepper. Simmer while covered for about 1.5 hours with occasional stirring.
  4. Bring a pot of water to boil. Cook the lasagna noodles for 8-10 minutes.
  5. Drain the noodles and rinse in cold water.
  6. Combine the ricotta cheese with the egg, remaining parsley, and 1/2 tsp of salt.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375F.
  8. To assemble: Spread 1 1/2 cups of sauce in a 9x13 baking dish. Put 6 noodles lengthwise, overlapping slightly. Cover with 1/2 of the ricotta mixture. Put 1/3 of the mozzarella. Put 1 1/2 cups of sauce over the cheese, and sprinkle with 1/4 cup of Parmesan. Repeat this and then cover with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan.
  9. Cover with foil sprayed with cooking spray to prevent cheese sticking.
  10. Bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes.
  11. Let it rest for 15 minutes before cutting.
